ULTRON Product Roadmap

Four connected hospital robotics products built on one data and autonomy platform. The roadmap starts with the working UV-C robot and progressively adds environment, workflow, patient-observation and medication-support capability.

iULTRON-UV-001 uses working operational records. ULTRON-Insight-002 uses controlled development data. Upcoming product reports use clearly identified simulation data.
ULTRON-UV-001Working

UV-C Guardian

Autonomous disinfection and foundational hospital operations data.

  • Mission runtime and coverage
  • Human-presence safety shutdown
  • Route, obstacle and occupancy metadata
  • UV lamp health and service records
