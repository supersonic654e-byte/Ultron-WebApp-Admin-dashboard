Planned Safety Architecture

Screening supportRules + reviewed signals

Present possible concerns to clinicians; do not issue an autonomous diagnosis.

Order controlDoctor/pharmacy approval

Only authorised hospital orders enter the delivery workflow.

Patient matchID + bed + order verification

Use multiple checks before unlocking a medicine compartment.

Audit trailPickup + route + handoff

Record who approved, loaded, transported and received each order.


Upgrade Recommendations

Use clinician-in-the-loop approval

No medicine should be selected or released without an authorised clinical order.

Add secure compartment and identity controls

Use electronic locks, role-based access and recipient verification.

Integrate pharmacy and EHR systems safely

Use approved interfaces, minimum necessary data and complete access logs.

Validate medication workflow hazards

Test wrong-patient, wrong-dose, duplicate-order, delay and unavailable-recipient scenarios.
